TRAFFIC & ROADWORK

Route 1 nighttime roadwork through Norwood

All of Norwood’s segment of Route 1 is affected: from the Walpole town line to the Westwood town line.

MassDOT notice

The Massachusetts Department of Transportation (MassDOT) is announcing that crews will be performing milling and paving operations on Route 1 northbound and southbound between the Walpole town line and the Westwood town line in Norwood.

Work will begin on Sunday, September 27, and continue through Friday, November 6. Work will occur during nighttime hours, Sunday through Thursday, from 8:00 p.m. to 5:00 a.m. the following morning.

During these operations, lane and shoulder closures will be required. At least one travel lane will remain open in each direction at all times.

Police details will be on site to assist with traffic management. Drivers traveling through the affected area should expect delays, reduce speed, and use caution.

All scheduled work is weather dependent and may be subject to change without notice.

Norwood.ma summary: the Walpole-to-Westwood limits described in the notice cover the full Route 1 segment within Norwood.
