Norwood Bar Crawl Bingo

Get four visited venues in a row, column, or diagonal. Your card mixes Norwood bars, breweries, and restaurants with active on-premises licenses.

A local bingo game with simple rules

This activity is for adults 21 and older. A venue visit is enough to mark a square: buying or drinking alcohol is never required. Follow each venue’s age and entry policies, check its current public hours, and arrange a safe ride.

The center is free. A line of five squares therefore takes four venue visits. Your card and marks stay in this browser on this device, on the honor system.
