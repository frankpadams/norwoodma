SAY HI — IT’S FRANK

I live here, too.

I built Norwood.ma because I live right here in Norwood and wanted it to be easier to find the information our community already has scattered across dozens of websites, calendars and organizations.

If you’re with a local organization, business, school, nonprofit or community group, I’m happy to talk about how your information can work with the site — whether that means adding your calendar or events, integrating an information feed, advertising or sponsorship, or just kicking around an idea for a new feature. I’m also happy to meet one-on-one in person here in Norwood.

And if you’re simply using the site and notice something that’s wrong, missing or could work better, please tell me. A lot of Norwood.ma gets better because somebody points something out.

ADVERTISE

Advertise on Norwood.ma

Want to reach a really local audience? We’re looking for Norwood-area businesses interested in advertising on Norwood.ma. Ads are clearly identified and kept separate from the way the site works — advertising never affects search results, directory placement, recommendations, or Dinner Spinner results.

That said, where an ad appears can matter. An ad on the Dinner Spinner and Food & Drink pages, for example, puts your business in front of people at the exact moment they’re trying to figure out where to eat. Other sections offer similar opportunities to reach people while they’re actively looking for something local.

Interested? Send me a message and tell me a little about your business. We can figure out what makes sense.

About contributions & advertising

Contributions are voluntary personal support for Norwood.ma. Norwood.ma is not a 501(c)(3) charitable organization, and contributions are not represented as tax-deductible donations.

Supporting Norwood.ma does not purchase preferential listings, coverage, recommendations, endorsements, or influence over what appears on the site.

Independent community information site. Not affiliated with the Town of Norwood government.

Inclusion of a news story, organization, business, event, resource, or external link does not constitute endorsement or support by Norwood.ma.
