Oct 16, 202610-16-2026 Attention Norwood Residents:

10-16-2026 Attention Norwood Residents: The MWRA is taking public comments until October 30 regarding proposed Combined Sewer Overflow (CSO) control plans, and our local water/sewer rates are on the line. While we support regional environmental goals, forcing Norwood ratepayers to absorb tens of millions of dollars for legacy sewer systems in other communities is fundamentally unfair. Norwood has its own separate sewer system and urgent local infrastructure to fund. Take action before October 30! Copy and paste the text below into the MWRA public comment portal to make your voice heard: I oppose the $1.3 billion CSO plan from MWRA, Somerville, Cambridge. The financial burden on the Norwood MWRA ratepayers is unfair. Debt service from the project costs $36 million in MWRA assessments to Norwood ratepayers ($109 million for the $7 billion plan advocated by some). We have our own water/wastewater projects that need capital investment. We have a stormwater infrastructure to improve. We don't get funding from MWRA to finance those projects. This is not a responsible solution. Documents, including a new letter from the Board of Selectmen, can be found on the Town's website: https://www.norwoodma.gov/government/committees_boards/board_of_selectmen/mwra_cso_plan.php Visit https://voice.somervillema.gov/joint-cso-planning for additional details. #NorwoodMA #NorwoodMass #NorwoodMACommunity

Sep 23, 2026September 23, 2026

September 23, 2026 🚨Protect your water bill 🚨 The Town of Norwood is encouraging residents to review and submit public comments regarding the Massachusetts Water Resources Authority’s (MWRA) proposed $1.3 Billion Draft Updated Combined Sewer Overflow (CSO) Control Plan. While this regional infrastructure initiative aims to address CSO discharges in the Cambridge, Somerville, and surrounding watershed areas, the current proposal shifts 60% of the project’s total cost to the broader MWRA regional system. Under this regional cost-allocation model, Norwood ratepayers would be responsible for an estimated $35 million+ in long-term debt service, despite receiving no direct local infrastructure improvements. The Norwood Board of Selectmen has formally submitted comments advocating for a re-evaluation of the funding allocation and a review of lower-cost alternative plans. We encourage you to submit comments. The CSO updated plan (as well as additional informative documents) can be found on the Town of Norwood website on the Selectmen’s page: https://www.norwoodma.gov/government/committees_boards/board_of_selectmen/mwra_cso_plan.php Public Comment Deadline: October 30, 2026 Submit Comments by Email: Comments can be emailed to the project team at UpdatedCSOControlPlan@aecom.com Submit Comments in the Online Comment Portal: Comments can be submitted to the project team electronically using the online comment portal hosted by Plan Engage at: https://us.planengage.com/updatedcsocontrolplan/page/home Submit Comments by Public Hearing: September 24, 2026 1pm-3pm (Virtual) register for the virtual meeting at: https://cbi-org.zoom.us/webinar/register/WN_iki7BcLZQfiCFu6UsmwuqQ#/registration October 28, 2026 6pm-8pm (Virtual or In-Person) Attend in person at Somerville City Hall Council Chambers, 93 Highland Ave., Somerville, MA Or register for the virtual meeting at: https://cbi-org.zoom.us/webinar/register/WN_T84wgkwMSeiCzLAOBdq16g #NorwoodMA #NorwoodMass #NorwoodMACommunity
