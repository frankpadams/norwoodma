PROPERTY-TAX WORK-OFF

Volunteer and reduce eligible property taxes

Norwood has long operated a senior tax work-off program, and recent town financial materials say the senior/veteran tax work-off program has been expanded. Current eligibility and benefit amounts should be confirmed with the Town.

🧓
Senior Tax Work-Off

Eligible older homeowners may volunteer in municipal departments in exchange for a property-tax abatement. Contact the Senior Center for the current application, limits and placements.
