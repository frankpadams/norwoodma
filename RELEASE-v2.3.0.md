## v2.3.0 — Norwood Pizza Passport and Bar Crawl Bingo

Released November 24, 2026.

- Adds the Norwood Pizza Passport, using the maintained restaurant records and including Mak’s Roast Beef & Pizza.
- Adds Norwood Bar Crawl Bingo with randomized 5×5 cards and a curated set of public venues whose active on-premises licenses are in the ABCC snapshot.
- Saves stamps and bingo marks in the visitor’s browser without an account. Certificates can be downloaded, shared, or printed after an honor-system attestation; the attestation is not printed.
- Adds both games to Games & Shenanigans, Discover, and the XML sitemap.
- Clarifies that bingo is for adults 21+, with no purchase or alcohol consumption required.
