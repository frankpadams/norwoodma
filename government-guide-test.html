Understanding Norwood Town Government

Who makes decisions in Norwood? How do departments, boards and Town Meeting work together? This independent guide explains the structure of local government and helps you find official information.

How town government works

Town Meeting

Legislative body. Norwood’s representative Town Meeting votes on matters including appropriations and bylaws, within the authority established by law and the town charter.

Explore Town Meeting and its archive →

Board of Selectmen

Elected executive leadership. The board exercises the powers assigned to it by the charter and state law, including oversight of the General Manager.

Find current members and meetings on the official town site →

General Manager

Chief administrative official. Norwood uses the charter title General Manager. The General Manager oversees municipal administration under the town charter and the Board of Selectmen’s authority.

What Public Works does

The Department of Public Works maintains much of Norwood’s public infrastructure. Its responsibilities include street and sidewalk maintenance, paving, pothole repairs, winter snow and ice operations, stormwater drainage, water distribution and sewer infrastructure, and waste and recycling services. Residents can use the resources below to find services, report problems, and review public information.

Common questions

How do I get a sticker for the Winter Street Recycling Facility?

Residents can obtain a vehicle sticker through the DPW administration office at 1 Lyman Place. Bring your vehicle registration and confirm current documentation requirements and office hours before visiting. The facility is at 370 Winter Street. Official trash and recycling information →

How do I report a pothole?

Contact Norwood Public Works with the street name, closest address or intersection, and a description of the problem. For an immediate traffic hazard, contact the appropriate emergency service. Direct reporting information is being verified.

What if my trash pickup was missed?

Check the Norwood.ma Comprehensive Trash and Recycling Guide for collection information. Contact the town's trash and recycling service for missed collections; current reporting instructions are being verified.

Where can I find recycling and disposal information?

See the Norwood.ma Comprehensive Trash and Recycling Guide or the official town trash and recycling page.

Who should I contact about a water or sewer problem?

Public Works handles municipal water and sewer infrastructure. For urgent hazards or a suspected major break, contact the town promptly. Current department phone numbers and after-hours instructions are being verified.
