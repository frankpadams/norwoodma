🔥 Natural gas

National Grid — Boston Gas

National Grid is the natural-gas distribution utility serving Norwood. Use National Grid for starting or stopping gas service, billing and current Massachusetts gas rates. If you smell gas or suspect a gas emergency, leave the area and use National Grid’s emergency instructions; call 911 for an immediate danger.

🌐 Internet, cable & TV

Norwood has multiple broadband choices, including Norwood Light Broadband, Xfinity and Verizon; wireless and satellite options may also be available. Exact technologies, speeds and prices vary by address, so provider address checks are more reliable than a town-wide advertised price.
