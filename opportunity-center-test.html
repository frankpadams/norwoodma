Fund a Community Project

Start with a project category, not a text search. Norwood's Community Preservation Act (CPA) funding is limited to specific statutory purposes, and proposals are reviewed by the Community Preservation Committee before eligible recommendations go to Town Meeting.

Affordable housing, including housing for veterans or people experiencing homelessness

Eligible community housing acquisition, creation, preservation and certain rehabilitation or support may qualify. Housing for veterans or people experiencing homelessness can be considered when it meets the CPA housing requirements. General services such as meals, counseling or ongoing shelter operations are not automatically eligible.

Historic buildings and preservation

Eligible historic resources may receive support for acquisition, preservation, rehabilitation or restoration, subject to statutory and local review.

Parks, playgrounds, recreation and open space

CPA supports qualifying open-space and recreation-land projects. Eligibility varies by acquisition, creation, preservation and rehabilitation purpose; not every playground improvement qualifies.

Music, arts, schools and community events

Explore the Norwood Cultural Council and Music Drives Us programs in Find Grants. Ordinary arts events are not a standalone CPA purpose.
