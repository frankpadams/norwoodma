# Norwood.ma v0.11.42

## Interactive Norwood Trivia

- Replaces the static trivia/local-facts page with a multiple-choice game.
- 152 sourced questions at launch, with a quality-first target of roughly 200.
- Random 10-, 25-, or 50-question rounds with shuffled choices, score, progress, answer explanations and source links.
- Topics include Norwood history, notable people, civic architecture, schools, industry, nature, archives, local geography and film/TV productions shot in Norwood.
- Adds a current local trivia-night section using verified recurring event information for Irish Brewing Boston and Castle Island Brewing.
- Adds the game under both Explore and Things to Do.
- Keyboard-accessible native controls and visible answer states.

## Research note
The bank is intentionally below the ~200 target in this release. The remaining questions should be added only after additional sourcing of census/demographic data, world records/unusual achievements, film-location details and other high-quality local material.
