CHECKLIST

Before and after you arrive

Before moving: compare housing, confirm school enrollment requirements, review transit, arrange utilities and internet, and identify any permits or town services you will need.

After moving: update voter/vehicle information as applicable, set up trash/recycling and utilities, explore local resources, calendars and community organizations, and learn the three commuter-rail stations serving Norwood.
