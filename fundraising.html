A Buck for Sally

Eagle Scout Liam Flanagan is raising funds to build a Comfort Corral at Coakley Middle School for Mustang Sally, the Norwood Police Department’s comfort dog. The planned enclosure includes benches, a doghouse, and storage for Sally’s toys.

How to contribute

Make checks payable to Norwood Public Schools and write The Comfort Corral in the memo field.

Mail to:
The Comfort Corral
C/O Paula’s Massage
1 Walpole St
Norwood, MA 02062

Venmo: @the-comfort-corral ↗

Luminary Night

Purchase by mailing payment to Norwood Circle of Hope, P.O. Box 421, Norwood, MA 02062; at Murph’s Place Restaurant, 58 Broadway; or using the order form included with November light bills. FCC Norwood supports Cradles to Crayons’ year-round Everyday Essentials program.
