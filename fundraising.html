Seasonal & Other Ways to Help

Girl Scout cookies and booth sales

Check ZIP code 02062 for upcoming booths. Official Cookie Finder ↗. Norwood troop-level links will be added when verified.

Car washes and school fundraisers

Monitor Selectmen agendas for municipal parking lot requests, then confirm approval and rain dates. Also check PTO newsletters, youth teams, school clubs, and Scouting announcements.

Restaurant fundraising nights

Watch Jake n JOES, the Chateau, and Texas Roadhouse for confirmed events benefiting Norwood groups, even when hosted outside Norwood.

Toys for Tots · Toy donation drop-offs

Find participating collection sites through the official Toys for Tots campaign locator. Choose Massachusetts and check the current campaign’s locations, dates, and accepted items before bringing donations.

Official Massachusetts campaign locator ↗

Cradles to Crayons · Clothing & shoe donations

Search the official drop-off locator using Norwood ZIP code 02062. Check the selected site’s hours and current donation guidelines. Cradles to Crayons accepts children’s clothing and shoes; its current guidelines exclude toys and baby gear. Bag or box donations and place them inside the collection bin.
