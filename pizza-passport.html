How it works

Mark a stop after you visit. There is no purchase requirement, account, or sign-in. Stamps are saved only in this browser on this device, on the honor system.

Earn certificates at 5, 9, and 13 stops. Your final self-attestation unlocks the certificate; it is not printed on the certificate.
