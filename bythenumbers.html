Reading the numbers

What a chart shows: A particular measurement for Norwood over a stated period.

What it doesn't show: Why a change happened, or whether one group of residents caused another outcome. Two trends moving together do not prove a relationship.

Check the details: Definitions, reporting periods, missing observations, and links to original sources are part of the evidence—not fine print.

How We Use Data

Accuracy before interpretation. Figures must be traceable to sources and reporting periods. Correlation is not causation. Research questions, interpretations and infographic claims remain unpublished until individually reviewed and approved. New data will trigger review rather than silently change an approved answer.
