Next review: Meadow Street river trails — source-based access and alignment checklist

Existing public paths, not merely a proposal. The August 2025 Meadow Street brochure states that trails are open seven days a week from dawn to dusk. The brochure filename contains “Prop,” but that is not evidence the pictured walking paths are only proposed.

Geographic control points to verify: trail entrance at the end of Meadow Street (also independently documented by the Neponset River Watershed Association); parking at the end of Meadow Street; the Neponset River bank; winding paths near the river; any junctions or crossings shown on the brochure. Condition: sections can flood after rain.

Independent reference: Neponset River Watershed Association recreation guide ↗ identifies walking trails at the end of Meadow Street near Norwood Airport. This supports the trailhead location, not the route linework.
Geometry status: brochure trail line tracing and control-point registration pending. Nearby OSM paths remain unverified candidates and must not be treated as the brochure route.
