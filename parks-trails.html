NORWOOD TRAILS

Maps, brochures & local trail guide

Use the Town’s Trails Advisory Committee document center for the official maps and brochures. The expandable list below gives you a quick sense of each trail before you go.

Norwood Trail Maps & Brochures

Official Town trail brochures, maps, preliminary maps and Trails Advisory Committee updates.

Bernie Cooper Park Neponset River · accessible riverfront walking

Riverfront open space created for passive recreation, with walking paths and views along the Neponset River. The Town’s trail document center includes its brochure/map.

Endean Park & Hawes Brook woods, pond & brook

A larger conservation area used for walking and passive recreation, with wooded trails around the Endean/Hawes Brook landscape.

Ellis Pond & Alevizos Park pond, benches & conservation land

Ellis Pond supports fishing and boating, while adjacent Alevizos Park is a small passive-recreation area with benches. The trail brochure ties the pond and park together.

Ellis Playground Trail recreation fields & neighborhood trail

A short local trail associated with the Ellis recreation area and nearby pond/open-space network.

Germany Brook woodland conservation trail

A wooded conservation-area walk following the Germany Brook landscape.

Hennessey Field & Murphy Park neighborhood woods & park connection

A wooded walking area linking Hennessey Field and the Murphy Park/field neighborhood.

Meadow Street Conservation Land quiet conservation walk

A conservation property with a mapped walking route through undeveloped open space.

Shattuck Park wooded walking trails

A roughly seven-acre wooded Town park used primarily for walking and passive recreation.

Vanderbilt / Pine Tree Forest multiple loops & 5K route

A trail network with several loop options; the Town publishes both an all-loops map and a mapped 5K route.

Willett / Tiot Trail school-area trail connection

A mapped trail segment near the Willett School and adjoining parkland, part of the developing Tiot Trail network.

Ledgeview Drive preliminary trail route

A developing/preliminary trail area for which the Town currently publishes a preliminary map.

Senior Center walking route mapped neighborhood walking loop

A mapped walking route centered on the Senior Center area for an easy local walk.

Fields, courts & outdoor fitness

Jean Brown Field plus a back field used for organized recreation.

Coakley Middle School athletic complex 1315 Washington St

Rectangular field, softball field, baseball field and tennis courts. Norwood Recreation also installed a public 9-hole disc golf course here in 2021; a 2022 Recreation guide described it as available outside conflicting athletic events. Because disc golf is not separately listed in the current 2026 MyRec facility directory, confirm current course availability with Recreation before making a special trip.

Coakley walking / cross-country route 1315 Washington St · around athletic complex

A walking and cross-country route runs around the Coakley athletic-field area, with connections toward the Hawes/Ryan Drive area. It is useful for walking and running when it does not conflict with school or athletic use.

Savage Center: exercise path, basketball & pickleball 275 Prospect St

Norwood Recreation’s 2024 park guide listed a walking path and outdoor exercise stations here. The Savage Center also has outdoor basketball and dedicated pickleball courts; the Town’s 2025 renovation project reconfigured and resurfaced the courts and planned expansion from three to four pickleball courts. Recreation’s 2026 pickleball classes are currently scheduled at the Savage Center Courts.

Disc golf — Coakley Middle School 1315 Washington St · status worth confirming

The Town added a public 9-hole disc golf course at Coakley in 2021, and Recreation’s 2022 guide said it could be used whenever it did not conflict with athletic events. It is not separately shown in the current 2026 online facility directory, so call Recreation at 781-762-0466 if course status is important to your visit.

Tennis courts Coakley / Ivatts · Hawes

Coakley Middle School’s Ivatts tennis courts at 1315 Washington St are currently listed as open in MyRec. Recreation guides have also listed tennis courts at the Hawes recreation area, 1305 Washington St; because Hawes is not broken out by court in the current facility directory, check Recreation for current court status before making a special trip.

Basketball courts Balch · Callahan · Cleveland · Murphy · Prescott

Balch’s basketball courts at 1168 Washington St are currently listed in MyRec. Recreation guides also identify basketball courts at Callahan, Cleveland, Murphy and Prescott; availability and condition can vary, so MyRec or Recreation is the best current source before organized use.

Pickleball Savage Center · outdoor courts

The Savage Center at 275 Prospect St has dedicated outdoor pickleball courts. Norwood Recreation’s 2026 programming is currently using the Savage Center Courts for pickleball instruction, and Town project reports document the recent pickleball/basketball court renovation there. The 2025 renovation plan expanded the layout from three to four pickleball courts.

Norwood High School indoor walking track 245 Nichols St · resident access permit/pass

The indoor walking track above the NHS gym is available to Norwood residents. Start with the School Department’s official Walking Track Access page and its linked “Walking Track Documentation.” The established access process has included an application, CORI/background check and a photo-ID access pass; because procedures and hours can change with school and athletic schedules, use the current documentation before visiting. For questions or current access instructions, contact the Norwood Public Schools Facilities Office at 781-440-5830.
