LAUNCH IN NORWOOD

Where to put in

Two confirmed local canoe/kayak access points, with practical parking information.

🛶
Ellis Pond & Alevizos Park

Two canoe/kayak launch locations. The Town brochure identifies parking on Walpole Street near the dam and on Nichols Street.

Town trail map & access details
🛶
Bade Canoe Launch

Public launch on the Neponset River behind the River Ridge office area off Route 1. Town open-space materials show a parking area and describe it as a canoe-launch and river-race staging area.

Town open-space guide
