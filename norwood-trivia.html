ABOUT THE QUESTIONS

Local facts, with receipts

The game currently draws on the Norwood Historical Society, Morrill Memorial Library's local-history archives, Town records and Norwood on Film. Categories include history, notable people, landmarks, schools, industry, nature, Norwood on screen and local oddities. Census and other changing facts will be date-labeled before they are added.
