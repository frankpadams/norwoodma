# Norwood.ma v1.4.4 — Facebook share paste guidance

**Release date:** October 27, 2026

## User-facing changes

- After choosing a Facebook profile or group, the quiz copies the score caption and link and downloads the score graphic.
- A confirmation popup explains that the caption and link are on the clipboard and should be pasted into the Facebook post or group, and that the downloaded graphic should be attached.
- Facebook opens only after the user selects “Open Facebook” in the popup.
