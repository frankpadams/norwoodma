# Norwood.ma v1.7.0

Released November 10, 2026.

- Collects official Town Facebook and Instagram posts once daily through the public embeds on the Town homepage.
- Shows dated posts, images and source links on Town Updates, groups matching posts and reports feed failures while retaining the last snapshot.
- Updates Get Involved with Recreation volunteer contacts, FY2027 senior work-off documents and Town board position notices. The expired May 1 Assessors notice is labeled with its deadline.

Validation covers duplicate posts, edited posts, failure retention, video-only posts, safe markup and date retention.
