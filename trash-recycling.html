Why Norwood.ma does not guess your route from your street name

The Town route map contains boundaries that can split longer streets. Use WM’s address lookup or locate your home on the official map rather than assuming every address on a street has the same schedule.

Weekly trash

WM provides weekly residential trash collection using a 64-gallon cart. Put the cart out by 7:00 a.m., wheels toward the curb, with at least 3 feet of clearance from parked cars, mailboxes and other obstacles.

Bag trash, do not overfill the cart, and keep the lid closed. Material outside the cart may not be collected or may incur an additional charge.

Every-other-week recycling

Norwood’s recycling calendar alternates between Red and Yellow recycling routes. Once you know your normal weekday and route color, the annual calendar tells you which weeks to put recycling out.

The Town route map is useful for locating your area, but it is detailed and includes split route boundaries; use the official address lookup when in doubt.

Holiday delays

Norwood’s 2026 calendar identifies New Year’s Day, Memorial Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas as holidays that delay trash and recycling pickup by one day. Always confirm the current Town/WM holiday notice for a specific holiday.

Missed pickup or cart problem?

If your cart was out on time but was not emptied, WM directs Norwood residents to report the missed pickup to the Town of Norwood: 781-762-1413.

Additional carts and replacement carts also go through the Town. WM provides an online path for cart repair requests.

Winter Street Recycling Facility

370 Winter Street · Saturdays, 8:00 a.m.–1:30 p.m. The facility is for Norwood residents and a residential vehicle sticker is required. Town/EPA materials say stickers are free with proof of residency and vehicle registration; obtain one through DPW at the John J. Carroll Administration Building, 1 Lyman Place.

The Town’s current program and recent official reports describe drop-off for leaf bags and brush, single-stream recycling, food scraps, metals, bulky rigid plastics, fluorescent bulbs and other mercury-containing products, textiles, books, Styrofoam packaging, mattresses/box springs, Christmas trees and other accepted bulk items. Free finished compost is also made available to residents when available.

Textile recycling

Norwood accepts textiles for recycling at the Winter Street Recycling Facility. Clothing and other accepted textiles can be diverted from the trash; check current Town guidance for accepted items and facility requirements before dropping them off.

Compost drop-off

Residents also have access to food-scrap compost drop-off through the Town program, in addition to the optional curbside service described below. Check the Town Trash & Recycling page for current compost drop-off locations and instructions.

Swap Shop

The Swap Shop at the Winter Street facility gives usable household items another life instead of sending them into the waste stream. Norwood DPW’s 2024 annual report confirms the Town continued operating the Swap Shop. Because acceptance rules and seasonal availability can change, check DPW’s current notices before bringing items specifically for the Swap Shop.

Bulk items & special recycling days

Winter Street has bulk-item and metal collection capacity for accepted resident material. The Town has historically limited quantities and has item-condition rules, so check current DPW guidance before loading a large item. Norwood also schedules special Household Hazardous Waste / recycling days. The 2026 calendar marks April 4 and August 29; on those dates the facility closes at 11:00 a.m. and leaf/brush disposal is not available.

Before you go: special-event rules, Swap Shop availability, accepted materials and facility operations can change. Check the current Town/DPW notice, especially for hazardous waste, mattresses, electronics, large appliances and unusual bulk items.

Curbside food-scrap composting

Norwood residents can subscribe directly to Black Earth Compost for curbside food-scrap collection. This is a separate, optional paid service rather than part of the regular WM trash/recycling program.

Current advertised pricing

Black Earth currently advertises weekly pickup for $15.99/month or every-other-week pickup for $12.99/month. Six-month options are shown as $84.99 weekly or $67.99 every other week. Pricing and plans can vary by address and may change, so verify your Norwood address before subscribing.

How to register

Use Black Earth’s Home Pickup page, enter your Norwood service address, choose an available pickup frequency and starter-kit option, and create your customer account. Black Earth says signup takes less than two minutes.

Check Norwood pricing & sign up ↗

How pickup works

Put the green compost bin at the curb by 6:00 a.m. on your assigned collection day. Black Earth accepts raw, cooked and spoiled food scraps and also materials that generally do not belong in a backyard compost pile, including meat, bones, dairy and certified compostable packaging. The company says participating households can receive a spring voucher for a free bag of finished compost, subject to its current program terms.

Optional add-ons

Black Earth currently sells curbside add-on stickers for $7 each (sold as five for $35) for eligible yard-waste, textile or compost-overflow pickup alongside the green bin. Limits and accepted materials apply. Check the current add-on page before purchasing.
