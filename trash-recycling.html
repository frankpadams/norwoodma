Trash, Recycling & Disposal Guide

Open the section you need.

Winter Street Recycling Facility

370 Winter Street · Saturdays, 8:00 a.m.–1:30 p.m. The entrance gate closes at 1:00 p.m.; vehicles must be inside before then. A Norwood residential vehicle sticker is required.

The facility accepts a broad range of resident material, including leaves and brush, single-stream recycling, food scraps, metals, rigid plastics, fluorescent bulbs and other mercury-containing items, textiles, books, white EPS/Styrofoam packaging, mattresses and box springs, Christmas trees and eligible bulk items. Finished compost is made available to residents when available.

Composting — free drop-off & curbside pickup

Free food-scrap drop-off

Norwood DPW’s residential food-scrap program lists drop-off at the Public Works Yard, 1 Lyman Place (daily, 7 a.m.–7 p.m.) and the Winter Street facility, 370 Winter Street (Saturdays, 8 a.m.–1:30 p.m.; residential sticker required). Town materials have also promoted additional school-based drop-off locations; check the current Town page before making a special trip because locations can change.

Home composting

DPW sells discounted compost bins at 1 Lyman Place and provides a countertop scrap bucket with bin purchases. The Town currently lists an Earth Machine and a dual composting tumbler; check the Town page for current pricing and availability.

Curbside service

Black Earth Compost offers optional paid curbside food-scrap pickup in Norwood. Enter your address on its residential pickup page for current service availability and pricing.

Fabric & Textile Recycling

Textiles are banned from Massachusetts trash disposal unless they are contaminated or otherwise unusable. Norwood says reusable or recyclable clothing, shoes, belts, linens, towels, curtains and other cloth items should not go in the recycling cart.

The Town provides textile drop boxes at Norwood public schools, the Savage Center and the Winter Street Recycling Facility. Items should be clean and dry. Ripped clothing and unmatched shoes are accepted. The former curbside “Pink Bags” textile service is no longer operating in Norwood.

Bulk Item Disposal

The Winter Street facility accepts eligible bulky items and metals. Mattresses and box springs may be brought there Saturdays from 8:00 a.m.–1:30 p.m.; the Town currently limits these to three items per week, requires a residential sticker, and requires them to be dry, clean and free of infestation.

For items requiring curbside handling or when you are unsure whether an item is accepted, check the current Town instructions or call DPW at 781-762-1413 before putting it out.

Leaf & Brush Pickup

DPW provides seasonal curbside leaf-bag and brush collection. The Town’s current trash guidance says residents seeking brush pickup should call DPW the day prior. Historical DPW guidance specifies paper leaf bags only and bundled brush; collection can be delayed by weather, equipment, emergencies and heavy seasonal volume.

Residents may also bring leaves and brush to the Winter Street facility. Keep leaf-compost material free of trash, rocks, dirt and other contaminants, and keep brush separate.

Swap Shop

The Town operates a Swap Shop at the Winter Street Recycling Facility so usable household items can be reused rather than discarded. Availability and accepted-item rules can change, so check current DPW notices before bringing an item specifically for the Swap Shop.
