Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, 2026

Polls are open 7 a.m.–8 p.m. Your Election Day polling place depends on your residential address. You must vote at your assigned location.

🕒 Early voting in Norwood

Early voting for the November 3, 2026 state election takes place at Memorial Hall, first floor of Norwood Town Hall, 566 Washington Street. The final early-voting day is Friday, October 30, from 8:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m.

⚖️ Voting problems or intimidation? Get help

If someone threatens, intimidates, obstructs, or improperly interferes with your ability to vote—including someone acting in a law-enforcement or military capacity—you can seek assistance or report the incident. The presence of authorized police at a polling place does not, by itself, mean interference is unlawful.
