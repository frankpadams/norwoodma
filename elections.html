Norwood, Massachusetts · State Election

Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, 2026

Polls are open 7 a.m.–8 p.m. Your Election Day polling place depends on your residential address. You must vote at your assigned location.

Enter your Norwood address in the Commonwealth's official lookup to see your assigned voting location and a sample ballot for your precinct. Don't assume your polling place is Town Hall.

Election Day reminder: If you're in line at your assigned polling place by 8 p.m., you can still vote. In-person early voting ends October 30; Election Day voting takes place at your assigned precinct location, not necessarily the early voting site.

🕒 Early voting in Norwood

Early voting for the November 3, 2026 state election takes place at Memorial Hall, first floor of Norwood Town Hall, 566 Washington Street. The final early-voting day is Friday, October 30, from 8:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m.
