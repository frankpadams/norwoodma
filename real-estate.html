NORWOOD, MASSACHUSETTS

Living in Norwood

Homes & Real Estate

Norwood combines the convenience of a Boston suburb with a strong community of its own. Situated between Boston and Providence, the town has three commuter rail stations and convenient access to Route 1, Route 1A and I-95/Route 128, making it easy to reach communities throughout Greater Boston and the region. At the same time, there is plenty close at hand: a lively town center and Town Common, restaurants, local shops and everyday services, arts and entertainment, community events, parks and recreation. Major shopping destinations and hundreds of additional restaurants, stores and services are a short distance away.

🏫SchoolsDistrict, programs and how to look beyond a single rating.›

Norwood Public Schools serves more than 3,400 students in Pre-K through grade 12, with an early-education program, five elementary schools, Coakley Middle School and Norwood High School. The district also operates Little Mustangs Preschool Academy.

Schools: Balch, Callahan, Cleveland, Oldham and Prescott elementary schools; Willett Early Childhood Center; Coakley Middle School; and Norwood High School. Families can use the district’s enrollment and registration resources for current assignment and registration information.

Norwood schools offer academics alongside arts, athletics and extracurricular programs. Rather than reducing a school system to a single rating, this page will point families to official district information, state data and the programs and opportunities available to students.

Why we chose Norwood for our family

Test scores and rankings can be useful context, but they do not tell the whole story. Academic outcomes also reflect family income, parental education, language and other factors outside a school’s control. Our family considered those numbers, but also programs, teachers, opportunities, diversity and our children’s day-to-day experience. Our children have thrived in Norwood Public Schools, and knowing what we know now, we would choose Norwood again.

🛡️Safety & Public SafetyPolice, community engagement and a factual look at public safety.›

Norwood Police provides patrol, investigative and community services. The department’s 2023 annual report recorded more than 4,000 foot patrols in neighborhoods, business districts and schools, and described a full-time clinician assigned through a Massachusetts Department of Mental Health grant to assist with mental-health calls and follow-up.

Norwood Police also works in the schools and community. Officer Geoffrey Baguma is partnered with Mustang Sally, the department’s Golden Retriever therapy/community resource dog, who regularly visits students and community events.

For a sense of day-to-day Norwood Police activity, the latest Norwood police log is available in the current edition of The Norwood Record ↗.

🚆Commuting & TransitCommuter rail, 34E bus and commute context.›

Norwood is served by three Franklin/Foxboro Line commuter-rail stations — Norwood Depot, Norwood Central and Windsor Gardens — plus MBTA Route 34E bus service. Route 1, Route 1A and I-95/Route 128 also run through or directly serve Norwood, giving residents several ways to reach Greater Boston and the wider region.

⚡Utilities & Everyday CostsElectricity, water/sewer, internet, trash & recycling — with nearby-town comparisons.›

Norwood’s municipal services can affect the real cost of living in town. Norwood Light is the Town’s community-owned electric utility and also operates Norwood Light Broadband. Its newer Broadband Fiber service uses a 100% fiber-optic network with symmetrical speeds; the fiber-to-the-home upgrade is still being completed, so availability should be checked by address.

Norwood also provides free public Wi-Fi on the Town Common, a service launched in partnership with Norwood Light.

Norwood receives MWRA water from the protected Quabbin and Wachusett reservoir system and distributes it through the Town’s locally maintained water system. The latest annual report covers 2025 drinking-water testing and was published in June 2026.
