NEIGHBORHOOD FOOD SHARING

Little Food Pantries

Small neighborhood food-sharing locations that provide an easy, low-barrier way to get food when you need it or share food when you can.

See Locations on a Map →
  • 100 Winter St — First Congregational Church Blessings Box; shelf-stable meal kits on the front steps, no registration required.
  • 280 Railroad Ave — Little Free Food Pantry; outdoor mini-pantry available for pickup at any time, with no eligibility requirement.
Need more food assistance? The Norwood Food Pantry at 150 Chapel Street and other food-support programs are listed under Food & Basic Needs.

These small pantries depend on community support and availability can change. Know another little food pantry in Norwood? Tell Norwood.ma →

And don’t forget the Morrill Memorial Library. These neighborhood book boxes are a wonderful, convenient resource for sharing books close to home, but Norwood’s full public library offers much more — books and media, programs, research help, museum passes, technology, a Library of Things and other services for the whole community.
