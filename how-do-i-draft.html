How do I change my mailing address?

If you are moving and want your mail forwarded, submit an official Change of Address with USPS. USPS notes that this only changes your mailing address with the Postal Service; government agencies, voter registration, banks and other organizations must be updated separately.

U.S. Postal Service — Change of Address & Mail Forwarding →

If you also need to change the mailing address associated with a Norwood property or Town account, update that separately with the appropriate Town office.

Become a poll worker

Poll workers are hired and trained by the local election office. If you want to work an election in Norwood, contact the Town Clerk’s Office and tell them you are interested in serving as an election worker. The Clerk’s office can tell you about upcoming elections, available assignments, training and current eligibility requirements.

Norwood Town Clerk: MaryLou Folan · 566 Washington Street · 781-762-1240 · mfolan@norwoodma.gov · Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–4 p.m.

Norwood Town Clerk — Voting & Elections →

The Massachusetts Elections Division also directs prospective poll workers to their local election office and provides a statewide directory.

Massachusetts — Find Your Local Election Office →
Become a Town Meeting Member

Norwood has a Representative Town Meeting, and Town Meeting Members are elected from the Town’s districts. If you are a new candidate, obtain nomination papers from the Norwood Town Clerk’s Office. For the 2026 Annual Town Election, new Town Meeting Member candidates needed signatures from 10 registered voters in their own Town Meeting district. Election dates and filing deadlines change each year, so confirm the current deadline with the Clerk before collecting signatures.

Incumbent Town Meeting Members use a different process: for the 2026 election, the Clerk mailed incumbents a Letter of Intent that had to be returned by the nomination deadline.

Norwood Town Clerk: MaryLou Folan · 566 Washington Street · 781-762-1240 · mfolan@norwoodma.gov · Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–4 p.m.

Town Clerk — Voting & Elections →

Before deciding to serve, you can also read Norwood’s official Representative Town Meeting rules. They explain meeting procedures, attendance, debate and voting. Only elected Town Meeting Members may make motions and vote, although registered Norwood voters may speak when recognized by the Moderator.

Norwood Representative Town Meeting — Rules of Procedure →

Recreation, Parks & Community Events

Register for a Community Garden plot

The Norwood Community Garden is a volunteer-run organic garden on Mylod Street in the Endean Park/Conservation area. Current information published in 2026 says the garden has 90 plots, generally 8 by 16 feet, with an annual $35 plot fee plus a $5 donation to the Norwood Food Pantry. Water, compost and topsoil are included, and gardeners participate in seasonal work parties and garden tasks.

To ask about an available plot or the waitlist, email: norwoodmacommunity-garden@gmail.com
