Utilities & Home Services

How do I set up my utilities?

Norwood uses different providers for different utilities. Set up each service that applies to your home; internet availability and technology can vary by address.

Electric: Municipal electric service is provided by Norwood Light Department.

Norwood Light Department — electric service →

Water & sewer: These are municipal services provided by the Town of Norwood.

Town of Norwood — Water & Sewer Services →

Natural gas: National Grid (Boston Gas) is the natural-gas distribution utility serving Norwood.

National Grid — Massachusetts Gas service →

Internet: Three principal wired/home-internet choices to check are Norwood Light Broadband, Xfinity and Verizon. Service type, speed and availability depend on the address.

How do I change my mailing address?

If you are moving and want your mail forwarded, submit an official Change of Address with USPS. USPS notes that this only changes your mailing address with the Postal Service; government agencies, voter registration, banks and other organizations must be updated separately.

U.S. Postal Service — Change of Address & Mail Forwarding →

If you also need to change the mailing address associated with a Norwood property or Town account, update that separately with the appropriate Town office.

How do I get something notarized?

Start with the Commonwealth’s official notary guidance. Massachusetts does not provide a public statewide searchable directory, but the state explains where to find a notary and how to obtain contact information for a specific commissioned notary.

Commonwealth of Massachusetts — Find a Notary Public →

Local option: Morrill Memorial Library offers notary service by appointment. Walk-ins are not available; the library says service is free for up to 10 notarized documents per family per month, with a fee thereafter.

Morrill Memorial Library — Notary Service & Appointments →
